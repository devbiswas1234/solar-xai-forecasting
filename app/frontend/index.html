Dominance of Direct Irradiance & Autoregression

Pyranometer and Power_Output_lag1 account for 81.4% of global feature attribution. Because photovoltaic conversion follows Beer-Lambert photon absorption, immediate irradiance paired with sub-hourly inertia creates the primary generation envelope.

2

Thermal Efficiency Penalty Derating

The engineered feature Temp_Diff = Temp1 - Air_Temp exhibits a negative SHAP gradient when module temperature exceeds 42°C. This cleanly captures the silicon semiconductor temperature coefficient (nominal -0.4%/°C power degradation).

3

Diurnal Harmonic Stability

Cyclical encodings (Hour_sin, Hour_cos) prevent gradient discontinuities at noon and midnight boundaries, providing a smooth diurnal baseline and suppressing false solar ramp alerts during low-angle sunrise/sunset.
