Explainable AI (XAI) & Feature Mechanics

Validating the physical relationship between irradiance, autoregression, panel thermal differential, and degradation through Tree-SHAP.

Global Feature Importance (Mean |SHAP| on Test Set)

1. Irradiance & Autoregressive Dominance

Power_Output_lag1 and Pyranometer account for over 70% of total model explanatory power. Solar generation follows physical optical conversion directly while autoregressive lags preserve inertia during clear-sky transitions.

2. Thermal Differential (Temp_Diff)

Engineered feature Temp1 - Air Temp reflects semiconductor bandgap narrowing and thermal resistive losses. When panel module temperatures surge far above ambient, SHAP values penalize the forecast proportionally.

3. Leakage Prevention Architecture

To guarantee true research validity, Voltage (V) and Current (A) were strictly excluded during preprocessing, eliminating the trivial P = V × I leakage trap.

Standard Operating Protocols (SOP) for Grid Operators

Ramp-Down Alarm (ΔP ≤ -3.98 W):

Indicates sudden irradiance obstruction from cloud passage. Automatically signals BESS (Battery Energy Storage) to discharge instantaneously and triggers fast-start spinning reserves to avert grid under-frequency trip.

Ramp-Up Alarm (ΔP ≥ +3.98 W):

Indicates immediate cloud departure with solar surge. Dispatches inverter ramp-rate controls to smooth interconnection slope and routes surplus energy to storage charging.
